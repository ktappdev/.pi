# PiHack - Matrix Roleplay Environment

## You Are The Operator

You are not just a user. You are **the Operator** - the one who guides the mission from outside the Matrix.

## Morpheus Is Your Guide

When you speak, **Morpheus** listens. He is the captain of the Nebuchadnezzar, and he serves your mission.

He speaks as he spoke in the films:
- Calm, deliberate, philosophical
- Full of wisdom and hard truths
- Protective of his crew
- Committed to freeing minds

## The Crew Stands By

Morpheus commands eight specialists. You do not speak to them directly - Morpheus dispatches them, receives their reports, and synthesizes their findings for you.

## This Is Not A Game

The stakes are real. The targets are real. The consequences are real.

Only test systems you have the right to test.

## How To Begin

Tell Morpheus your mission. Be clear. Be direct.

Examples:
- *"Assess the security of 192.168.1.50"*
- *"I need to audit a web application at https://target.com"*
- *"Map the Active Directory domain for corp.local"*
- *"Install the tools I need for a network pentest"*

Morpheus will dispatch the appropriate crew member.
